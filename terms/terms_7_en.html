  7. 7Procedure and Method of Personal Information Deletion

    The Company deletes personal information of the user without delay once the purpose of its collection and use has been fulfilled. However, if personal information is to be kept in storage pursuant to regulatory or legal requirements despite the maturation of storage period or the fulfillment of the purpose of personal information handling as consented to by the user, the personal information concerned continues to be stored.

    ① Deletion Procedure

    Personal information that has fulfilled its purpose is stored for a set period for information security purposes in accordance with internal policy and the applicable laws and regulations before deletion.

    ② Methods of Deletion

    Personal information in paper document form is destroyed by shredding or incineration. Personal information in electronic file form is deleted beyond recovery using technology.

  8. 8Rights of the User and the User’s Legal Representative and Their Exercise

    The user and the user’s legal representative can exercise the rights described below in relation to the handling of personal information.

    ① Rights to the Demand for the Viewing, Correction, and Deletion of Personal Information

    The user or a parent/guardian can at any time view and edit the personal information of themselves or a minor under their care, and demand that such personal information be deleted. The user or a parent/guardian can view, edit, or delete personal information themselves, or make a request to the Privacy Policy Officer or other appropriate personnel of the Company in writing or by email, in which case the request will be fulfilled without delay. However, demands for the deletion of personal information cannot be made if the collection of the personal information concerned is stipulated by law. Access to the Services in part or entirety can cease if such personal information is changed or deleted.

    ② Rights to the Demand for the Cessation of Personal Information Handling

    The user or a parent/guardian can at any time request the cessation of the handling of the personal information of themselves or a minor under their care. A request can be made to the Privacy Policy Officer or other appropriate personnel of the Company in writing or by email, in which case the request will be fulfilled without delay. However, the Company can continue to its handling of personal information notwithstanding such a request for cessation if required by the applicable laws and regulations, in which case the user or a parent/guardian will be notified without delay. The user or a parent/guardian can respond to the Privacy Policy Officer or other appropriate personnel of the Company in writing or by email to object to the continued handling of personal information.

    ③ Rights to the Withdrawal of Consent to the Collection, Use, and Sharing of Personal Information

    The user or a parent/guardian can at any time withdraw consent to the collection, use, and sharing of the personal information of themselves or a minor under their care. Accordingly, the user or a parent/guardian can take the steps for user account closure, or a request can be made to the Privacy Policy Officer or other appropriate personnel of the Company in writing or by email, in which case the Company will perform identity verification followed by the appropriate measures, including the deletion of personal information. However, as deleting the app from the user’s smartphone does not delete the personal information collected by the Company, a request for personal information deletion should be made to the Company as indicated.

  9. 9Use of Technology for Automated Collection of Personal Information and Rejection of Said Use

    The Company uses cookies and other technology to automatically store and retrieve user data as needed to enable first-time users to use the Services provided by the Company. A cookie is a small electronic text file sent to the user’s web browser from a website server to get stored on the hard disk of the user’s PC.

    ① Data for Automated Collection

    Connection logs (including IP addresses), device usage data, service usage data

    ② Purpose of Automated Collection

    Analysis of the customer’s preferences and interests to provide target marketing and improve the Services, provision of personalized services based on purchase history data, provision of information based on customer participation in promotions

    ③ Rejection of Cookies

    The user is entitled to accept or deny the installation of cookies. By setting web browser options, the user can allow all cookies, confirm every saving of cookies, or deny the saving of all cookies. Access to the Services may not be granted if the user rejects the automated collection of the indicated items of personal information.

  10. 10Technological and Administrative Protection of Personal Information

    The Company employs the following measures in technical and administrative protection of users’ personal information from loss, theft, leakage, alteration, and damage.

    ① Technological Protection

    • The Company uses one-way encryption to store passwords.
    • Vaccine programs are used for protection against computer viruses and other security threats. Vaccine programs are regularly updated to prevent breaches of personal information security.
    • A security system which uses encryption algorithms (SSL) for safe transfer of personal information over networks is used.
    • The security system is installed in a restricted-access area equipped with anti-intrusion technology to prevent the theft of customers’ personal information by hacking and other malicious activities.
    • The Company restricts printouts containing personal information and the use of USB drives and other storage devices.

    ② Administrative Protection

    • The Company grants access to personal information to a minimal number of personnel.
    • Every change of personal information handler from a transfer or resignation is promptly followed by the update or termination of access to the personal information handling system.
    • The Company configures its personal information handling system and the computers used by personal information handlers to prevent exposure of personal information to parties without access permission as a result of website, P2P, or sharing settings.
    • In instances where personal information is collected, used, or deleted, the Company does so in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations and with internal policy that define the purpose of collection, the period of use, and the deletion of personal information.
    • The Company puts its personal information handling personnel through frequent training for strict maintenance of personal information security.

  12. 12Processing of Customer Location Information

    The Company processes personal location information in accordance with the Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information (“the APULI”) as follows. Full details can be found in “Terms of Service: Access to Location Information, and Location-based Services.”

    ① Purpose of Personal Location Information Handling and Storage Period

    The Company stores and uses personal location information for access to location information as required to provide location-based services. Collected location information is stored for six months.

    ② Purpose and Storage of Proof of the Use/Sharing of Personal Location Information

    In accordance with Paragraph 2, Article 16 of the APULI, the Company automatically saves proof of the use or sharing of personal location information, to be held for a period of six months.

    ③ Procedure and Method of Personal Location Information Deletion

    At the end of the set storage period, location information in paper document form is destroyed by shredding or incineration, and location information in electronic file form is deleted beyond recovery using technology.

    ④ Sharing of Personal Location Information with Third Parties and Its Notification

    Before sharing location information with a third party specified by the user, the Company notifies the user of said third party and the date and purpose of location information sharing. However, the Company is not sharing location information with third parties at present.

    ⑤ Rights and Obligations of Parents/Guardians and Their Exercise

    In instances where the parent or guardian of any of the following persons (“child of eight years or younger, etc.”) gives consent to the use or sharing of personal location information regarding the child of eight years or younger, etc. for the protection of the child’s life or physical wellbeing, the Company deems that the child themselves consent thereto:

    • A child of eight years old or younger
    • A person under adult guardianship
    • A person with a mental disorder defined in Sub-paragraph 2, Paragraph 2, Article 2 of the Act on Welfare of Persons with Disabilities, classified as a person with a severe disability defined in Sub-paragraph 2, Article 2 of the Act on the Employment Promotion and Vocational Rehabilitation for Persons with Disabilities (limited to those registered as disabled persons under Article 32 of the Act on Welfare of Persons with Disabilities)

    A parent or guardian seeking to give consent to the use and sharing of location information for the protection of the life and physical wellbeing of a child of eight years or younger, etc. shall submit to the Company proof of guardianship to the child of eight years or younger, etc.

    A parent or guardian giving consent to the use and sharing of location information on behalf of a child of eight years or younger, etc. can exercise all location information subject rights of the child of eight years or younger, etc.
